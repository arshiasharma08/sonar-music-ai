07 How it works

Audio Features

Analyzes objective audio characteristics like energy, danceability, tempo, and valence from structured music data.

Recommendations

Uses weighted Euclidean distance to find songs with similar audio-feature profiles, returning tracks most related to your selection.

Mood Classification

Groups songs into mood categories based on energy and danceability patterns, making it easy to filter by vibe.

Song Comparison

Compares two tracks side-by-side across all audio features, highlighting which aspects make them similar or different.
